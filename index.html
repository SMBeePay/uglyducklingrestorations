Meet the Restorer

A Decade of Bringing Furniture Back to Life

For well over ten years, restoring furniture has been more than a job — it’s a craft she’s poured herself into piece by piece. What started as a love for rescuing overlooked, worn-down furniture has grown into Ugly Duckling Restorations: a small, family-run shop built on patience, hand skill, and an eye for what a piece can become.

She’s also a mom to three boys — and that same hands-on care shows up in every piece, whether it’s carefully matching a stain, tightening a wobbly joint, or giving a forgotten find a whole new life in someone’s home.

Our Craft

The Craft Behind Every Piece

Every piece we create draws on the same core skills — from structural rebuilding to hand-applied finishes.

Hand Refinishing

Stripping, sanding, and refinishing to bring out natural wood grain with a durable, beautiful top coat.

Structural Rebuilding

Re-gluing joints, replacing broken parts, and rebuilding structure so every piece is sturdy for everyday use.

Custom Stains & Finishes

Color-matched stains and hand-applied finishes, from rich traditional tones to modern whitewash and matte looks.

Antique & Vintage Restoration

Careful, respectful restoration of antique and vintage finds that keeps their history and character intact.

Hardware & Detail Work

Sourcing and restoring pulls, hinges, and hardware, plus veneer repair and other detail finishing touches.

Painting & Whitewashing

Hand-painted, distressed, and whitewashed finishes for a farmhouse or vintage-inspired look.
