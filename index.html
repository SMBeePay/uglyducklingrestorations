Meet the Restorer

A Decade of Bringing Furniture Back to Life

For well over ten years, restoring furniture has been more than a job — it’s a craft she’s poured herself into piece by piece. What started as a love for rescuing overlooked, worn-down furniture has grown into Ugly Duckling Restorations: a small, family-run shop built on patience, hand skill, and an eye for what a piece can become.

She’s also a mom to three boys — and that same hands-on care shows up in the shop, whether it’s carefully matching a stain, tightening a wobbly joint, or bringing a family heirloom back to something worth passing down again.

  • 10+ years of hands-on restoration experience

What We Do

Restoration Services

Every piece is different, so every restoration is treated on its own merits — from a light refresh to a full rebuild.

Furniture Refinishing

Stripping, sanding, and refinishing to restore natural wood grain and a durable, beautiful top coat.

Structural & Joint Repair

Re-gluing loose joints, replacing broken parts, and rebuilding structure so furniture is sturdy for everyday use again.

Custom Stains & Finishes

Color-matched stains and hand-applied finishes, from rich traditional tones to modern whitewash and matte looks.

Heirloom & Antique Restoration

Careful, respectful restoration of family heirlooms and antiques that keeps their history and character intact.

Hardware & Detail Work

Replacing or restoring pulls, hinges, and hardware, plus veneer repair and other detail finishing touches.

Painting & Whitewashing

Hand-painted, distressed, and whitewashed finishes for a farmhouse or vintage-inspired look.
